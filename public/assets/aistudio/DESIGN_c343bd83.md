## Brand & Style

This design system channels unapologetic early-2000s McBling glamour: the iconic era of rhinestone-encrusted flip phones, pink velour tracksuits, holographic CD sheens, and glossy high-gloss excess. The target demographic embraces nostalgic digital kitsch, camp luxury, and high-energy personal expression.

The visual execution combines hyper-tactile glossy skeuomorphism with retro digital candy aesthetics. Interfaces feel touchable, lacquered, and radiant—using high-specular glass highlights, metallic silver chrome bevels, jewel-toned depth, and radiant starburst accents (✦ ✧ 💖). It departs from flat minimalism in favor of dimensional exuberance, lush saturation, and playfully luxurious ornamentation.

## Colors

The palette revolves around vibrant hot bubblegum pinks contrasted against cool liquid-chrome silver and plush velvet depths:

- **Primary (`#FF007F`)**: Saturated neon bubblegum magenta. Powers primary interactive controls, hero branding, active states, and glossy candy fills.
- **Secondary (`#C0C0C0`)**: Reflective liquid chrome and rhinestone sheen. Used for metallic borders, bevel gradients, and high-shine pill trims.
- **Tertiary (`#FFB6C1`)**: Soft baby pastel pink. Operates as structural wash, secondary pill containers, subtle gradient transitions, and plush highlight layers.
- **Neutral (`#1A0926`)**: Deep glam plum-velvet. Replaces standard grays and blacks for ultra-high-contrast text, jewel-case borders, and nocturnal structural grounding.

### Surface and Holographic Gradients
- **Candy Glaze Surface**: Vertical linear gradient from white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`) at 0%, through primary pink (`#FF007F`) at 45%, to deep fuchsia (`#D10068`) at 100%.
- **Holographic DVD Iridescence**: 135-degree dynamic linear gradient alternating `#FFB6C1` (baby pink), `#E6E6FA` (holographic lavender shimmer), `#C0C0C0` (chrome), and `#7BF0FF` (subtle holographic cyan glint) at high transparency (15–30%).
- **Velour Foundation**: Warm light-blush background (`#FFF0F5`) across light viewports, transitioning to plum velvet (`#1A0926`) for dark accent wrappers.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the experimental, wide-set glamour of **Syne** for loud display moments with the clean, rounded friendliness of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for body readability.

- **Syne** provides the signature early-2000s Brat/McBling energy: expansive uppercase forms, dramatic curvatures, and bold attitude. Use Syne for all display banners, page headers, badges, button triggers, and tab headers.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** balances Syne by providing crisp legibility inside chat modules, dense feeds, metadata lists, and form inputs.
- **Styling Rules**:
  - Main display headings may feature an inset metallic or drop-glow text shadow: `0px 2px 0px #FFFFFF, 0px 4px 12px rgba(255, 0, 127, 0.4)`.
  - Uppercase transformations are mandatory for `label-lg` and `label-md` to mimic rhinestone-studded merchandise tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout system is built on a responsive 12-column grid for desktop views, collapsing to 6 columns on tablet, and 4 columns on mobile. 

- **Desktop (>= 1024px)**: Outer margin is `margin-lg` (3rem), column gutter is `gutter-lg` (2rem). Content cards and jewel-case modals can either float centered or pin to full-bleed gloss frames.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: Outer margin scales to `margin` (1.5rem), column gutter is `gutter` (1.25rem).
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: Outer margin compacts to `margin-sm` (1rem), column gutter to `gutter-sm` (0.75rem).
- **Rhythm & Structure**: Component padding uses chunky, cushioned intervals. Internal button padding defaults to `space-sm` vertical and `space-lg` horizontal to retain the pillowy, plump velour profile.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy abandons flat gray drop shadows, executing depth through multi-tiered skeuomorphic treatments: candy gloss, metallic bevels, and vibrant neon aura lighting.

### Layer 1: Plump Surface Elevation (Cards, Containers)
- Base containers sit on an opaque light blush or frosted lavender plate bordered by a dual-line chrome stroke: a 1.5px outer border tinted `#C0C0C0` paired with an inner white specular highlight (`inset 0 1px 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`).
- Shadow layer combines a crisp 1px offset with an electric pink aura: `0 4px 0 #D10068, 0 12px 24px -4px rgba(255, 20, 147, 0.25)`.

### Layer 2: Tactile Bevel & Emboss (Buttons, Interactive Badges)
- Buttons utilize an explicit 2000s candy glaze highlight: an inset top gradient (`inset 0 2px 2px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`) and an inset bottom bevel shadow (`inset 0 -2px 3px rgba(120, 0, 60, 0.5)`).
- Pressed states physically depress downward: removing bottom drop shadows, shifting translate Y by `2px`, and darkening the inner rim glaze.

### Layer 3: Rhinestone Edge & Starburst Accents (Modals, Hero Popovers)
- High-level floating components display an iridescent rim shadow (`0 0 0 2px #E6E6FA, 0 0 16px rgba(255, 182, 193, 0.6)`).
- Dialogs feature a layered glass backdrop filter (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px) saturate(180%)`) topped with a faint pink glitter/starburst particle gradient.

## Shapes

The design system uses high roundedness (`level 3` / Pill-shaped). Sharp geometry is completely avoided in favor of voluptuous, plump, and friendly contours inspired by vintage flip phones, cosmetics compacts, and Bratz accessories.

- **Buttons & Input Badges**: Full pill caps (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to produce the classic early-aughts bubble bar aesthetic.
- **Cards & Panes**: Pill-softened corners using `rounded-xl` (3rem) on outer enclosures, creating comfortable, cushion-like containment.
- **Thumbnails & Avatars**: Circular (`50%`) with double-ring chrome and hot pink rhinestone borders, or squoval (`2rem`) when holding wide media previews.
- **Accents**: Secondary ribbon tags and chips leverage asymmetrical teardrop pills or scallop-edged borders reminiscent of stickers and diary badges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Glam Button**: Pill-shaped (`border-radius: 9999px`), hot bubblegum pink gradient fill (`#FF007F` to `#D10068`). White text set in uppercase Syne with bold drop shadow. Layered top half-oval gloss glare (`linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(255,255,255,0.7) 0%, rgba(255,255,255,0) 50%)`). Finished with a 1.5px solid silver chrome border and tactile pink bottom drop shadow.
- **Secondary Chrome Button**: Pill-shaped, silver metallic linear gradient fill (`#FFFFFF` to `#C0C0C0`). Plum-velvet text (`#1A0926`). Inset specular light rim with hot-pink hover glow.
- **Icon Buttons**: Circular candy domes embedded with starburst (✦), heart (💖), or sparkle glyphs, complete with physical push-down travel on `:active`.

### Chips & Badges
- Ultra-compact pills padded with `space-xs` vertical and `space-sm` horizontal.
- Pale baby-pink or holographic lavender background with a 1px dashed or bedazzled dotted chrome outline (`border: 1.5px dotted #FF007F`). Text in `label-sm` with decorative trailing sparkle glints.

### Cards
- Plump cards constructed with a 2-piece structure: a pearlescent frosted upper panel and a saturated bubblegum footer or header band.
- Outlines incorporate a 2px composite stroke: metallic chrome on the exterior with baby pink rim lights. Cards exhibit a subtle 3D tilt and pink glow transition on hover.

### Inputs & Text Fields
- Deep pill-shaped fields filled with ultra-pale rose tint (`#FFF5F8`).
- Inset depth shadow (`inset 0 2px 4px rgba(26, 9, 38, 0.12)`) replicating physical recessed plastic casing.
- Border shifts on `:focus` to high-voltage fuchsia (`#FF007F`) with a 4px soft bubblegum outer glow ring (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 4px rgba(255, 0, 127, 0.25)`). Placeholder text set in soft plum-lavender.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkboxes**: Squoval pill containers (`border-radius: 6px`) in chrome silver. Checked state bursts into hot pink with a custom metallic white heart icon (♥) replacing the checkmark.
- **Radio Buttons**: Concentric metallic rings containing a baby-pink glossy gem that illuminates hot fuchsia when selected.

### Lists & Navigation Bars
- Navigation tabs evoke flip-phone status trays: horizontal pill carousels nested inside a semi-transparent lavender frosted dock.
- Selected tab items use glossy candy bubble fill with embossed labels; unselected tabs remain transparent with muted plum typography.

### Specialized Accents
- **Glitter & Starburst Glints**: Floating CSS pseudo-elements (✦, ✧) strategically positioned at card corners and primary CTA apexes, pulsing with a brief sparkle animation.
- **Leopard Print Accent Ribbons**: High-contrast, stylized stylized pink-on-magenta leopard print patterned strips utilized sparingly for section divider banners, notification borders, or card headers.
